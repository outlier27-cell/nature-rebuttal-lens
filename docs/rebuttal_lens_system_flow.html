Nature RebuttalLens System Flow

A manuscript-aware, cross-disciplinary Author Rebuttal Assistant workflow over Nature transparent peer-review interaction cases. The system outputs traceable planning artifacts, not final submission-ready rebuttal text.

Inputs

Reviewer comment or review excerpt
Manuscript text / Markdown / LaTeX-like text
Optional draft author response
Optional editor letter
Optional retrieved Nature case analogies

Hard Boundaries

No acceptance prediction
No fabricated evidence
No unsupported commitments
Author confirmation required
Case analogies are not rules
Layer 0: Manuscript Context context boundary
1. manuscript_context_extractor

Builds a section inventory and records what the supplied manuscript text can and cannot support.

↓
Layer 1: Review Understanding concern and risk
2. reviewer_understanding_agent

Extracts explicit reviewer concerns and observable textual evidence.

3. tacit_concern_interpreter

Interprets tacit risks without claiming private reviewer psychology.

4. manuscript_evidence_locator

Maps concerns to supplied manuscript evidence, partial support, missing evidence, or uncertainty.

↓
Layer 2: Strategy and Evidence bounded planning
5. institutional_signal_interpreter

Identifies journal, transparency, community, and editor-facing signals without predicting outcomes.

6. case_retrieval_interpreter

Uses retrieved Nature cases as bounded historical analogies.

7. evidence_action_planner

Plans evidence actions, required artifacts, and author-confirmation questions.

↓
Layer 3: Author Response Planning stance and commitment
8. author_positioning_agent

Offers response stance options while preserving author agency.

9. tone_commitment_calibrator

Flags defensive tone, sycophancy risk, overclaim, and unsupported commitments.

↓
Layer 4: Cross-Disciplinary Interpretation humanistic lens
10. actor_network_mapper

Maps human and non-human evidence carriers: figures, datasets, code, tables, supplements, methods text.

11. cross_disciplinary_lens_interpreter

Applies tacit knowledge, institutional dependence, actor-network alignment, fast/slow correction, tone/commitment, and author-agency lenses.

↓
Layer 5: Integrity Gate release-safe output
12. integrity_adequacy_checker

Checks adequacy, provenance, unsupported claims, responsible-use warnings, and author-confirmation requirements.

↓
Outputs traceable artifacts
rebuttal_lens_trace.json
rebuttal_lens_summary.json
Concern map and tacit risk notes
Manuscript evidence map
Evidence action plan
Author confirmation questions
Tone and commitment warnings
Integrity and adequacy report
